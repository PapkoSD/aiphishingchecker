How it works

Two models run on your computer. One reads the wording of emails and text messages, the other looks at the structure of links. Both were trained on public collections of real phishing and legitimate messages. On top of the models, the checker compares links with a list of official websites, spots look-alike domains such as paypa1.com, and checks whether the sender's name, address and authentication match.
